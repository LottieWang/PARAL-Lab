Course policies

The final syllabus will be posted on D2L. Grading weights and any schedule-dependent adjustments are still being finalized.

Academic integrity

Students must follow DePaul’s academic integrity policies. Submit work you understand and can explain, cite sources, and acknowledge permitted assistance. Do not copy or distribute another student’s solutions or present someone else’s work as your own.

Keep assignment code and solutions in the private course repositories. Follow each handout’s rules for collaboration and outside resources. Ask the instructor when the permitted use of a resource is unclear.

AI tools

The final syllabus and individual assignment instructions will specify permitted AI assistance and any disclosure requirements. Do not assume that a tool is allowed simply because it is available.

Grading

Assessment is planned to include written work, coding exercises, a midterm, and two projects. The final syllabus will specify percentage weights, rubrics, and any changes to the coursework plan.

Submission deadlines

The current submission plan provides a two-calendar-day late window. Gradescope will display the ordinary deadline and the hard cutoff for each applicable assignment. Check the final syllabus and each handout for exceptions and any late penalties.

For code submissions, your report identifies the exact repository commit to grade. A later push does not update an already-submitted report. Both the report and its referenced pushed commit must satisfy the applicable cutoff.

Enrollment dates

The standard university deadline to drop Fall 2026 classes with no penalty is September 22; the withdrawal deadline is October 27. Any course-specific exception requires an official university decision. Consult the DePaul academic calendar and your academic advisor.

Getting help

Start assignments early and leave time to debug and run experiments. Use D2L for course announcements and email lwang107@depaul.edu for course questions. Office-hour details will be announced.
