Welcome to CSC 503!

How do we turn additional processors into faster algorithms? This course connects the analysis of shared-memory parallel algorithms with their implementation and evaluation in C++.

Topics include work and span, sequence operations, sorting, concurrency, graph algorithms, parallel data structures, and scheduling. You will reason about correctness and efficiency, then examine how those ideas perform on multicore hardware.
