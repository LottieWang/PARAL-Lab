Presentation sign-up

Sign up on the shared spreadsheet. Pick one paper from the list below and enter your name and DePaul email next to it on the presentation sign-up sheet. One presenter per paper, first come first served: if a paper already has a name, choose another one.

How to sign up

  1. Find a paper on the sign-up sheet whose Presenter cell is empty.
  2. Fill in Presenter (full name) and Email (DePaul email).
  3. Do not edit or remove anyone else's entry. If two people end up on the same paper, the instructor will assign the later one another paper.

Every paper has a PDF link. If you would like to present a paper that is not on the list, email the instructor first.
